# Third-party components

- **ExifTool 13.59**, by Phil Harvey: https://exiftool.org/ . Runs as a separate executable. Official Windows x64 archive includes Perl and dependencies; their notices are preserved in `exiftool/exiftool_files/LICENSE` and `Licenses_Strawberry_Perl.zip`. Upstream source: https://github.com/exiftool/exiftool/tree/13.59 and https://sourceforge.net/projects/exiftool/files/Image-ExifTool-13.59.tar.gz/download . ExifTool is distributed under the same terms as Perl (Artistic License or GNU GPL); consult the bundled component licenses.
- **c2patool 0.28.2**, Content Authenticity Initiative: https://github.com/contentauth/c2patool/releases/tag/v0.28.2 . Separate unmodified executable; MIT or Apache-2.0 licenses are included in `c2patool/LICENSE-MIT` and `LICENSE-APACHE`. Includes the upstream SDK and its cryptographic dependencies. The official sample `sample/C.jpg` is used solely as a test fixture and is not part of the portable application.
- **nlohmann/json 3.12.0**, Niels Lohmann: https://github.com/nlohmann/json/tree/v3.12.0 . Header compiled into the application; MIT notice is included in `JSON-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Microsoft Visual C++ runtime**, `vcruntime140.dll`, copied from Visual Studio's x64 Redistributable directory and installed beside c2patool. The main GUI uses the static CRT; the upstream c2patool binary imports this DLL. Microsoft redistribution documentation: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/redistributing-visual-cpp-files?view=msvc-170 . No runtime installer is executed.
- **C2PA Conformance Trust List**, https://github.com/c2pa-org/conformance-public/tree/main/trust-list . The local public certificate snapshot is used for offline certificate-chain trust, not as an identity database or current online revocation source. Its date and digest are recorded in `DEPENDENCIES.json` and exported validation results.

The application uses Windows-provided Win32, Common Controls and CNG APIs. Downloaded parser runtimes are not analysis inputs and are never selected by inspecting a file.

- **DeviceKit model-name facts**, snapshot 19528aa07e1426626ea9fb2c4a161edf43c4f9e8: https://github.com/devicekit/DeviceKit/tree/19528aa07e1426626ea9fb2c4a161edf43c4f9e8 . The derived iPhone/iPad/iPod hardware identifier/name relationships are included in the offline catalogue. MIT notice is preserved in `data/DEVICEKIT-LICENSE.txt`; no upstream template or Swift code is executed by this app or the catalogue generator.
- **Google Play supported-device model/name facts**, snapshot 2026-10-23: https://storage.googleapis.com/play_public/supported_devices.html , referenced by https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/1727131 . The offline catalogue retains factual retail-brand/model/marketing-name relationships only, not images, specifications or device codenames. Source fingerprints and scope are in `data/device-models.sources.json`. The upstream list can change; the app never fetches it at runtime.
